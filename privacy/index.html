Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Andrey Kublitskiy (“we”) builds AuditPress, a Chrome extension. This policy describes what the extension does with data.

What the extension does

AuditPress scans web pages for accessibility issues, records manual accessibility checks, and creates reports. Scans run only when you start them, on the page you choose.

Site access

  • When you click the AuditPress toolbar icon or use its keyboard shortcut, Chrome lets AuditPress read the current tab so it can run a scan there.
  • If you choose “Allow AuditPress on <site>”, Chrome lets AuditPress scan that one site and its embedded frames without clicking the toolbar icon again. You can remove a site at any time in AuditPress Settings or in Chrome’s extension settings.
  • AuditPress never asks for access to all websites, and it does not run on pages you have not scanned.

Data that stays on your computer

The extension stores projects, scans, findings, manual check results, screenshots of page elements, reports, settings, and a cached license status in Chrome’s local extension storage on your computer. A count of reports you have exported may also be kept in Chrome sync storage, which Chrome syncs only if you have turned on Chrome sync.

None of this is uploaded to our servers. We do not operate an application backend. Page content, URLs, screenshots, and accessibility findings never leave your browser through this extension. Reports and backups are saved only to files you choose to download.

Screenshots can contain personal information shown on the page you scan. They stay on your computer. Settings include an option to blur text in screenshots and to delete old screenshots.

Data handled by payment providers

Paid plans are billed by ExtensionPay through Stripe. If you start checkout or restore a purchase, ExtensionPay and Stripe process the email address and payment details you give them. To calculate sales tax or VAT, checkout also asks for your billing country and, where required, postal code or address, and lets businesses enter a VAT or tax ID. We use these only to charge the right tax and to keep the tax records the law requires. The extension asks ExtensionPay whether your license is active; that request does not include page data. We do not receive your card number.

  • extensionpay.com/privacy
  • stripe.com/privacy

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads
  • We do not sell or share page data
  • We do not use analytics or telemetry in the extension
  • We do not run content scripts on the websites you visit; code runs on a page only when you start a scan or a check tool there
  • We do not send page HTML to a cloud accessibility API

Chrome Web Store limited use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
