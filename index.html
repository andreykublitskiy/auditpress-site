Accessibility audits in a Chrome side panel

Run a real accessibility audit in an hour, not a day. Scans run entirely in your browser; no page content leaves your computer.

AuditPress scans the page you are on against WCAG 2.2, 2.1 or 2.0 Level A and AA, guides you through the manual checks automation cannot do, and shows which criteria were tested and with what result.

Scanning, manual checks and on-screen results are free, and so are your first 3 full branded reports. Paid plans are billed through Stripe via ExtensionPay. Saved scans and reports stay on your computer and are never deleted when you change plans.

Automated testing finds only some accessibility issues. AuditPress does not certify compliance and is not legal advice.
